Signing in

Open the admin page and enter your admin token. It's the password for the whole system — keep it in a password manager, not on a note by the desk.

What the token protects

Anyone holding it can see every customer's name, phone number, email and home address. Don't email it, don't text it, and don't reuse it anywhere else. If you think it's got out, ask for a new one — it takes a minute to change.

Closing the browser tab signs you out.

New to the admin page?

Press Tour at the top right for a one-minute walkthrough of every part of the page. It runs by itself the first time you sign in.

Setting up the days you're available

You publish days, not classes. A day is: the date, which course, the hours you're willing to work, how many people you'll train that day, and the price per person.

  1. Click Add days
  2. Course name and type — CPR / AED, Heartsaver, or Babysitter Safety
  3. Your open hours — either Weekly hours or One day (below)
  4. People you'll train that day — your limit across every visit, based on manikins, cards and how much you want to do in one day
  5. Base price per person, and the group price bands (below)
  6. How many trips you'll make into each travel zone per day (below)

Customers then book four-hour sessions inside those hours, at their address. They only ever see start times that fall inside your open hours — closed days and closed hours never appear.

Set your hours once: the repeating schedule

In Add days, tick the courses you'll teach and set each one's price, then tick the days you're open and set the hours — say Monday to Friday 9:00 to 5:00. Leave For how long on Keep repeating and choose how far ahead to keep open (8 weeks is a good start). That's it: new days are added by themselves every day, so the calendar never runs out.

The schedule shows in the Repeating schedule box at the top of the admin page, where you can edit, pause or delete it. Editing rebuilds the upcoming days nobody has booked; booked days keep the hours and price the customer booked. Want Babysitter Safety on Saturdays only? Add a second schedule just for that.

For a one-off, choose One day, or a set number of weeks in For how long.

Trainers

Each trainer can do one visit at a time, whatever the course. Set how many trainers you have on a normal day under Booking rules. Each week in the list of days shows each date down the left with its trainers underneath: use − and + right there to change that day. To set a whole week at once, use Trainers for the week at the top of that week. Set a day to 0 and it takes no new bookings. If you lower a day below what's already booked, you're told which day so you can move or cancel a visit.

Course prices

Each course has one price per person, set under Course prices. It's the same every week: change it and every upcoming day of that course changes at once, and the booking page shows the new price straight away. Customers who already booked keep the price they paid.

Time off

Use Add time off for vacations, sick days or anything else. Nothing is offered on those dates. Days already there with nobody booked are removed; if someone has booked, you're told who so you can contact them.

Booking notice

Set how far ahead customers must book (24 hours to start). Times sooner than that aren't offered, so nobody books tomorrow 8am at 11pm tonight.

Travel zones

Distance is measured in a straight line from ZIP 97116 (Forest Grove) to the customer's ZIP, and sets their zone and travel charge:

ZoneDistanceTravel charge
Zone 10 – 25 milesNone
Zone 225.01 – 50 miles$50
Zone 350.01 – 90 miles$100

Beyond 90 miles the customer is told they're outside the area and asked to call. The charge is once per visit, not per person, and is added to the total before they pay.

On each day you can set trips per zone — for example 1 for Zone 3, so you're never sent to opposite edges of Zone 3 on the same day. Leave a zone blank for no limit, or put 0 to skip that zone that day. The limit is per calendar date: if two days fall on the same date, their trips count together. A customer in a zone that's used up for the day sees no times and is shown other days that can still reach them.

Group pricing

A visit costs the same to drive to and set up whether you're training two people or eight. So the per-person price moves with the group size:

Group sizePriceWhy
1–2 people+10% A small booking carries a share of the fixed cost of the trip
3–4 people−5% A fuller session spreads that cost further
5 or more−10% Best rate — one visit, more people trained

Customers see the adjusted price and the reason for it before they pay, so nobody is surprised at checkout. If someone is booking for two, the page also tells them what a third person would bring the price down to.

You set these bands per day when you create it, and the starting values are already filled in. Change a percentage, or the wording customers read, in the Group price bands table on the day's form. A small preview underneath shows what 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8 people would each pay.

Every group size needs a band

The bands have to run continuously from 1 upward, and the last one is left open-ended. The form will tell you if there's a gap — otherwise a group that falls through it would quietly pay the base price.

How your day fills up

Each booking takes four hours of teaching plus ninety minutes to set up, pack down and drive. So one booking blocks five and a half hours of your calendar.

8:00 – 12:00Booked — teaching at the customer's site
12:00 – 1:30Blocked — packing up and driving
1:30 PMNext time a customer can book

An 8:00 AM booking removes every start from 8:00 through 1:00 PM. The next offered time is 1:30 PM, which finishes at 5:30. So an 8–6 day holds two visits.

Want three visits in a day?

You'd need to open earlier or close later — three bookings need about fifteen and a half hours. Set the day's hours to whatever you're actually willing to work and the system offers what fits.

Two things run out separately

Times — how many four-hour sessions can still start that day.
People — the headcount you set for that day.

A day with hours free can still turn away a group of eight if only four places remain. The admin table shows both.

Seeing who's booked

Click Roster on any day. It's laid out as your run sheet for that day, in driving order.

Each visit shows the arrival time, the address as a link that opens Google Maps, any access notes the customer left (gate code, parking, which door), everyone attending, and what they've paid.

What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
confirmed Paid and locked in. This one is happening.
held Part-way through booking. Their slot is reserved for 20 minutes while they pay. If they don't finish, it frees up by itself.
offered Was on the waitlist, a slot opened, and it's reserved for them for 24 hours while they decide.
waitlist Waiting for a slot on a full day. Not booked, not charged.

Everyday jobs

Someone paid you cash or by cheque

Open the roster, find them, click Mark paid. They go to confirmed and their seat is secure.

Someone cancels

Remove next to their name frees just their place. On a group booking, cancel this visit in the visit header cancels all of them. Either way the freed places are offered to anyone waiting, automatically.

You need to call off a whole day

Edit the day, then Cancel this day. Existing bookings stay on the list so you still have their details — you need to phone those customers yourself. The system won't do it for you.

Changing a day's hours or headcount

Edit it. You can raise the headcount any time, and extra places are offered to the waitlist straight away. You can't drop it below what's already booked — the system will stop you rather than let you strand someone who paid.

Emailing everyone on a day

Copy contact emails at the bottom of the roster puts them all on your clipboard, ready to paste into a message.

What customers experience

  1. They pick a day from your schedule
  2. Choose how many people
  3. Enter the address you're coming to — as soon as they type the ZIP they see their zone and travel charge
  4. Pick an arrival time — only open times are shown
  5. Enter each person's name (everyone needs their own, for their card)
  6. Check the total — classes plus travel, itemised
  7. Pay — one payment covers the whole group and the trip
  8. Get a confirmation with a reference and an Add to calendar button

If a day is full they can join the waitlist, and they're also shown other days that would work — with the time they wanted listed first where possible.

Group bookings

A manager can book several people at once — a daycare signing up staff, for example. One address, one payment, one visit, but every person is named separately so they each get their own certification card.

Any attendee can optionally give their own email to get their own confirmation. Otherwise everything goes to whoever booked.

Groups appear on the roster as one block, so you can see at a glance that six names are one stop rather than six.

If something looks wrong

  • The page won't load — the NAS may have restarted. It normally comes back on its own within a minute or two.
  • A booking looks stuck on "held" — leave it. It clears itself after 20 minutes and the slot returns.
  • A customer says they paid but isn't confirmed — check the roster first. If they're genuinely missing, don't rebook them blind; their payment reference will sort it out.
  • Anything else — note the day, the customer's name and roughly when it happened. That's usually enough to track down.
